# Step 5A live-collection gaps

Live collection remains prohibited. The target date does not waive any item.

## Decisions requiring owner approval

- Final questionnaire and all three consent translations after linguistic,
  legal and privacy review.
- Participant eligibility/age policy and safeguarding process.
- Retention, withdrawal-receipt expiry, alternate withdrawal verification,
  privacy contact and appeals.
- Production architecture, vendor, approved data region, budget and support
  tier.
- Threat model, DPIA/privacy assessment and political-opinion handling basis.
- KMS, key custody, rotation/destruction and break-glass owners.
- RPO, RTO, backup retention, restore evidence and disaster-recovery owners.
- Identity provider, MFA, staff roles, training, offboarding and audit review.
- Abuse controls and a policy that does not claim one-device/one-person proof.
- Statistical analysis plan, disclosure thresholds and publication review.
- Incident response, breach notification, monitoring and on-call ownership.
- A separate explicit launch approval plus all five operational approvals.

## Engineering not yet implemented

- No HTTP intake route, Worker, Pages Function, API hostname or CORS policy.
- No production database/KMS/secret, private network or durable adapter.
- No authentication provider, admin interface, raw-data viewer or export job.
- No provider backup, deletion processor, restore environment or monitoring.
- No live browser form or persistent response storage.
- No external AI provider; political and confidential response data are
  prohibited from AI processing.

## Required launch evidence

Independent security review, privacy/legal sign-off, translation sign-off,
durable integration/load tests, access review, KMS exercise, successful restore
and deletion suppression, log inspection, disclosure review, operational
runbooks, rollback rehearsal and signed approvals must all be recorded before
any endpoint is connected to the public preview.
