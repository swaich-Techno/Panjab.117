The official AAP page states that its first list of 40 candidates was issued by its national secretary. The list itself is published as imagery on that page; the supplied six names were cross-checked against [The Tribune's contemporaneous report of the list](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/mann-jailed-minister-arora-among-40-mlas-renominated-by-aap-in-first-list/) before entry. The official party URL remains the primary source record.

The Satwant Singh record is deliberately not labeled “officially announced by party.” The Tribune reported an announcement by Akali Dal (Waris Punjab De), but a direct primary announcement and current ECI registration status were not verified. The organisation is therefore labeled cautiously and the candidate status remains “Reported/proposed, not confirmed.”

## Update policy

Each change must append, not overwrite, status history with an effective date, source URL, verification date and uncertainty note. Allowed statuses are:

- Officially announced by party.
- Reported/proposed, not confirmed.
- No verified announcement.
- Withdrawn/replaced, with evidence.

Before publication or any later launch, recheck each primary source and look for replacements, withdrawals, alliance arrangements, nomination filings and Election Commission updates.
