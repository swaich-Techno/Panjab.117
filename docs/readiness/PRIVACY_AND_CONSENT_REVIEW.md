# Privacy and consent review package

Status: engineering draft; qualified Indian legal/privacy and English consent
review pending.

## Draft notice elements for review

- Purpose: study local civic priorities across the nine Assembly segments; not
  an election, campaign, representative poll or prediction.
- Eligibility: at least 18, current resident in exactly one listed segment,
  self-submission.
- Voluntary participation: optional questions may be skipped and participation
  may stop without penalty or effect on services or benefits.
- Data: selected segment and structured civic answers; optional political
  answers only after separate consent.
- Preview boundary: the public preview transmits and saves nothing.
- Withdrawal: an approved release would provide a verified channel and
  withdrawal material; future processing stops and deletion starts subject to
  lawful limits.
- Retention: exact periods remain unapproved and must be tied to documented
  purposes, legal review and tested deletion.
- Contact: verified controller/privacy contact details are absent and must be
  published before collection.
- Methodology: voluntary responses are not representative.
- Coverage: English-only recruitment can exclude residents.

## Law and policy review

The final DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025. Rules 1, 2 and 17-21
commenced on publication; Rule 4 commences after one year; Rules 3, 5-16, 22
and 23 after eighteen months. On the target date, the phased status must be
confirmed by counsel against the official Gazette:
https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf

Counsel must also review the DPDP Act commencement notifications and
corrigendum, Information Technology Act obligations, CERT-In directions and
six-hour incident reporting, processor contracts, transfers/jurisdiction,
retention/log requirements, privacy-contact/rights procedures, and election
publication restrictions. ECI states restrictions for opinion/exit-poll
publication during specified election periods:
https://www.eci.gov.in/faq/5/16

Required recorded decisions are legal/privacy, consent wording, methodology,
security, backup/recovery, operations and accessibility. No approval may be
inferred or fabricated.
