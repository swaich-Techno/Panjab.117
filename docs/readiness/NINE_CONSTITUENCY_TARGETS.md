The same government page distinguishes the nine Assembly segments in the
Fatehgarh Sahib parliamentary constituency from the three Assembly
constituencies in Fatehgarh Sahib district. The project must not describe all
nine as part of Fatehgarh Sahib district.

Configuration constants:

- `TARGET_VALID_SUBMISSIONS_PER_CONSTITUENCY = 500`
- `NUMBER_OF_CONSTITUENCIES = 9`
- `TOTAL_MINIMUM_TARGET = 4500`
- `MAX_SUBMISSIONS_PER_CONSTITUENCY = null` (unlimited)

Targets are operational monitoring goals. They are not party quotas, sample
caps, representative sample sizes or permission to collect.
