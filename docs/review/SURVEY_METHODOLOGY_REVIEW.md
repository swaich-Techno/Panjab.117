## Exact English questionnaire

1. `assembly_segment` — Which Assembly segment do you live in or know best?
   - Choose one of the nine segments in the Fatehgarh Sahib parliamentary constituency.
   - 54 Bassi Pathana (SC); 55 Fatehgarh Sahib; 56 Amloh; 57 Khanna; 58 Samrala; 59 Sahnewal; 67 Payal (SC); 69 Raikot (SC); 106 Amargarh.
2. `local_infrastructure` — How would you rate local infrastructure in this area?
3. `roads_transport` — How would you rate roads and public transport in this area?
4. `healthcare_access` — How would you rate access to healthcare services?
5. `education_access` — How would you rate access to education and skills training?
6. `employment_opportunities` — How would you rate local employment opportunities?
7. `agriculture_livelihoods` — How would you rate support for agriculture and local livelihoods?
8. `public_services` — How would you rate access to everyday public services?
9. `community_priority` — Which one issue should receive the most attention locally?
10. `representative_responsiveness` — How would you rate the responsiveness of elected representatives to local concerns?
    - This optional political-opinion question is shown only after separate consent.
11. `public_decision_factors` — Which public issues matter most when you assess elected representatives? Select up to three.

Common rating options: **Very good; Good; Fair; Poor; Very poor; Prefer not to answer**.

Priority options: **Roads and public transport; Healthcare access; Education; Employment opportunities; Agriculture and local livelihoods; Public services; Water and sanitation; Community safety; Another issue; Prefer not to answer**.

Agriculture also offers **Not applicable to me**. Political questions are
optional and separately consented. The multi-select political question excludes
“Prefer not to answer” because the entire question can be skipped.

## Review questions

- Does “live in or know best” combine populations that must be analysed
  separately?
- Are constructs sufficiently defined to make rating scales comparable?
- Does question order create priming or political-consent pressure?
- Is “Fair” understood consistently in Punjabi, Hindi and English?
- Should community priority permit “another issue” without free text, or would
  that option be analytically misleading?
- Is the proposed 18+ population justified, and how will coverage error be
  described?
- What recruitment frame, mode, duplicate policy and non-response analysis are
  required?
- Are weights scientifically defensible? If not, prohibit weighting and all
  representative claims.
- What minimum segment sample and uncertainty disclosure are required before
  any release?
- Which cross-tabs are prohibited because of disclosure and sparse-cell risk?

## Required publication protocol

Approve a preregistered analysis plan, exclusion rules, denominator definitions,
missing-answer handling, duplicate-review cutoff, small-cell threshold,
complementary suppression, revision policy and plain-language limitations. No
candidate preference or election prediction is in scope.
