## Exact English participant notice

- **Participant information and consent**
- **Voluntary participation:** Taking part is voluntary. You may skip optional questions or stop at any time without penalty. Choosing not to participate will not affect any public service, benefit or relationship.
- **Purpose:** The proposed survey would study local civic priorities across the nine Assembly segments of the Fatehgarh Sahib parliamentary constituency. It is not an election, campaign or representative opinion poll.
- **Data collected:** If separately approved for launch, the civic path would collect your selected Assembly segment and structured answers about local services and priorities. Optional political-opinion answers would be collected only after separate consent.
- **Preview limitation:** This development preview does not transmit or save answers, identity, contact details, IP-derived identifiers or political opinions.
- **Anonymous response:** Anonymous response means no name or contact detail is attached. A one-time withdrawal code would be needed to locate it later; without that code, deletion of a specific anonymous response may be impossible by design.
- **Identifiable response:** An identifiable response would use optional contact details stored separately from answers and retained for a shorter approved period. This option is not implemented in the preview.
- **Retention:** Proposed limits: survey answers for no more than 12 months after collection closes; optional contact details for 30–90 days; deletion and consent evidence only for an approved compliance period. Exact periods require legal and privacy approval before launch.
- **Withdrawal:** You would be able to withdraw consent through a verified privacy-request channel as easily as consent was given. Future processing would stop and an approved deletion workflow would begin. Processing already completed lawfully before withdrawal would not be undone.
- **Privacy contact:** Privacy contact: verified details must be published before launch. No request channel is open in this preview.
- **Civic consent:** I have read the participant notice and voluntarily agree to preview the civic questionnaire. I understand that nothing is transmitted or saved.
- **Political section title:** Separate optional political-opinion consent.
- **Political section explanation:** These questions concern elected representatives and public decision-making. They are optional, more sensitive than the civic questions, and may be skipped without affecting the rest of the survey.
- **Separate political consent:** I separately agree to preview the optional political-opinion questions.
- **Decline political questions:** Continue without political-opinion questions.
- **Results limitation:** Any future results would describe voluntary responses only. They must not be presented as representative polling or an election prediction.
- **Draft warning:** Draft translation: human linguistic and legal review is required before publication.

## Proposed participant and age policy

The conservative proposal is adults aged 18 or older only, confirmed through a
single self-attestation. Do not collect date of birth, identity document or
electoral-roll data merely to prove age. This policy is **not approved**. Counsel
and the survey methodologist must decide whether excluding younger residents is
lawful, ethical and methodologically justified and specify how accidental minor
participation is handled.

- exact retention triggers, deletion exceptions and legal holds;

- incident/breach assessment and notification ownership; and
- whether public aggregate releases create unacceptable reidentification risk.
