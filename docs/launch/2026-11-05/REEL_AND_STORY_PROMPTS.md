# Reel and Story production prompts

Use 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, navy/ivory/muted-gold styling and the approved logo. Keep all text inside a 120 px side and 180 px top/bottom safe area. Add captions; do not imply responses are open.

## 10-second Reel

- 0.0–2.0 s: approved logo fades in. Text: “ਪੰਜਾਬ 117 / PANJAB 117”.
- 2.0–5.0 s: title: “ਫ਼ਤਿਹਗੜ੍ਹ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਲੋਕ ਸਭਾ ਹਲਕਾ / Nine Assembly Segments”.
- 5.0–8.0 s: “ਬਹੁਭਾਸ਼ੀ ਵੈੱਬਸਾਈਟ ਝਲਕ / Multilingual website preview”.
- 8.0–10.0 s: “ਜਵਾਬ ਹਾਲੇ ਸਵੀਕਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ / Responses not yet accepted” and `panjab-117.pages.dev`.

Audio: restrained instrumental bed only, properly licensed or platform-cleared; no political speech or crowd sound.

## 30-second Reel storyboard

- 0–3 s: logo on ivory; gold rule animates in.
- 3–8 s: headline in Punjabi, then Hindi, then English: Fatehgarh Sahib parliamentary constituency.
- 8–14 s: clean numeric sequence `54 · 55 · 56 · 57 · 58 · 59 · 67 · 69 · 106`; label it “nine Assembly segments,” not district constituencies.
- 14–20 s: website frames for Punjabi, Hindi and English. Overlay: “Read-only survey preview.”
- 20–25 s: navy card: “Collection postponed pending privacy, security and participation approval.”
- 25–30 s: URL, `@panjab.117`, “Independent Civic Research,” and “No responses accepted today.”

Voiceover order: Punjabi first, Hindi second, English third. Use a human-approved script; never synthesize a real person’s voice without permission.

## Three Story messages

1. **ਪੰਜਾਬ 117 — ਫ਼ਤਿਹਗੜ੍ਹ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਲੋਕ ਸਭਾ ਹਲਕਾ — ਨੌਂ ਵਿਧਾਨ ਸਭਾ ਹਲਕੇ.** Website preview available.
2. **ਬਹੁਭਾਸ਼ੀ ਖੋਜ ਝਲਕ:** Punjabi, Hindi and English. Independent and nonpartisan.
3. **ਸਰਵੇਖਣ ਹਾਲੇ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ।** Participation dates will follow privacy and security approval. Link sticker: `https://panjab-117.pages.dev/`.

## Visual-generation prompt

“Create a premium minimalist vertical civic-research launch background at 1080 × 1920. Deep navy field, warm ivory panels, restrained muted-gold rules, generous whitespace, subtle abstract map-grid geometry without geographic claims, no people, no ballot box, no party symbols, no flags, no logos and no text. Leave safe empty areas for a separately supplied approved logo and human-typeset Punjabi, Hindi and English copy. High contrast, editorial, nonpartisan.”

The generated background must be reviewed for accidental symbols or text. Add the approved logo and all copy manually; do not ask a generator to reproduce Gurmukhi, Devanagari, official IDs or the logo.

## Motion prompt

“Animate the approved static layout with 8–12 frame gold-line reveals, 300 ms ivory-panel fades and gentle 2% scale movement. No flashes, rapid zooms, political imagery, crowd scenes or fabricated UI. Reserve the last five seconds for a static URL and ‘Responses not yet accepted’ message. Provide a reduced-motion cut using only crossfades.”
